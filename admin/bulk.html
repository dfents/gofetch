Bulk add / price domains

Paste one row per line. Publishing here commits straight to data/domains.json on the live site — it goes live within a minute or two, same as any other change.

Format: domain, price, pricingMode, category|category, saleType
Only the domain is required. pricingMode is buy_now, make_offer, or private (defaults to buy_now if a price is set, otherwise make_offer). saleType is optional — type liquidation to show it in the Liquidation & current deals section.

Example:
niceword.com, 99, buy_now, Brandable, liquidation
otherword.io, , make_offer, AI
thirdword.net
