About

A collection, not a marketplace.

GoFetch is operated by the Fenton / Butcher partnership — a privately owned portfolio of domain names built through deliberate acquisition over time.

Most of what we hold isn't for sale in any urgent sense. Some names we'll part with readily; others we're content to sit on indefinitely, because the right use for them hasn't arrived yet, or because we simply like owning them. GoFetch exists to make that portfolio legible — a proper front door for it — rather than to move inventory.

Where a name suits a buyer's plans, we're straightforward to deal with. Where it doesn't, we'd rather say so than pretend otherwise. Buy now means exactly that. Make offer means we're open to the right number. Privately held means we're not actively selling, but a serious enquiry is always welcome.

The collection spans single words, compact brandables, and a handful of deliberate domain hacks — acquired individually, over years, rather than assembled as a batch. What's shown publicly is a working subset. If you're building something specific and can't find a fit, tell us — a relevant name may simply not be listed yet.

Have a specific brief?

Describe what you're building and what you'd consider paying. We'll check the full inventory, not just what's public.
